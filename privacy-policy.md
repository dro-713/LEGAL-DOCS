## What information we collect

Road Signs EE is built to collect as little as possible. The app does not require a login to study signs or request access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone for that purpose.

**Local progress and preferences:**
The app stores favorite signs, quiz history/scores and preferences (theme, language) locally.

**Sent to our backend:**
To determine which sign categories are free and show the partner-school list, the app fetches configuration from our Google Apps Script backend. School-code validation and applicable event reporting still use Google Sheets/Apps Script.

The app does not require a customer account or ask for your name or identity document to study signs. If you contact us, your message may contain your name, email address and purchase or support details. The legacy school-code service records the code, a temporary random session identifier and relevant creation, expiry or event times. These session/event records can be linked to one another and are treated as pseudonymous data, not guaranteed anonymous data. A 45-minute validation expiry does not delete a stored row. Partner-link events may record the school, action and event time; a report that shows totals does not mean only totals were stored.

**Purchases:**
Google Play processes Android purchases. The app uses Google Play purchase information, such as a purchase token, product identifier and purchase state, to unlock or restore access. For purchase support and refunds, we may also consult the order information available to us in Play Console, such as the order number, purchase date, amount, currency and payment/refund status. We do not receive your full payment-card details. This does not mean we automatically receive your Google-account email address.

Older app versions can also send a Google Play purchase token, product identifier and optional school session identifier to the legacy service for verification, and report unlock/purchase-related events. These requests are separate from the app's Play Billing grant and restoration of paid access.

**Technical/network data:**
Requests reach Google's infrastructure, which may process IP addresses and other network/security metadata under its own terms. This does not mean the Apps Script handler receives or records the client's IP address.

Use Android settings to clear the app's data if you want to remove locally stored progress and preferences. Uninstalling alone may not remove copies restored through Android or Google backup. Google may process Android/Play stability and technical information under its own terms; developers can receive diagnostics through Play Console where that service makes them available. This is separate from data transmitted by the app's own code or SDKs.

## Why we process this information

- To validate partner codes and provide or restore access, including Google Play purchase support and refunds
- To show current category and partner information
- To assess use of the partner program; totals can be derived from timestamped and linkable records
- To handle support and privacy requests

## Who we share information with

- **Google** — backend hosting (Google Sheets/Apps Script), Google Play Billing and platform services, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Support correspondence is handled through our email service, Zoho Mail.
- We do not sell or rent information to advertisers or data brokers.

## Data retention

Our retention rule for linkable legacy session and event records is one year from creation or the event; due records are handled in the monthly owner review, subject to unresolved obligations. Non-personal aggregate totals may be retained.

This is the adopted rule, not confirmation that historical rows, logs or backup copies have already been removed. Session expiry does not erase stored records. Purchase evidence and support records are retained as necessary for access, support, unresolved obligations and applicable legal retention duties; requesting deletion does not erase records that must lawfully be retained.

and isn't directed at young children. We don't knowingly collect information from
